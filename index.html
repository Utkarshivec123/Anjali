A little letter

My Anjali, if I had to describe you in one line, I'd fail — because you're the kind of person who needs a whole page, and even then I'd be leaving things out.

You turn ordinary days into ones I actually want to remember. Your laugh is unfairly cute, your hugs fix bad moods instantly, and somehow you make "just texting" feel like the best part of my day.

So today isn't really about flowers or teddies (though I got you plenty here) — it's just an excuse to remind you how lucky I feel that you're mine.

— Utkarsh
